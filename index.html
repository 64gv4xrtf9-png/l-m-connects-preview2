Website Design FAQ

Common questions about our £50 static website design service.

How does a £50 website work?

You pay a one-time £50 fee. We design, build, and publish your static website based on your requirements. There are no monthly fees or subscriptions.

What’s included in the £50 price?

A fully designed static website, mobile-friendly layout, basic SEO structure, publishing support, and full ownership of the final site.

Are there any ongoing costs?

No. Once the website is delivered, it’s yours. Any hosting or domain costs are separate and paid directly by you.

Who is this service for?

This service is ideal for small businesses, trades, startups, and service-based companies that need a simple, professional website.

How do I add my own domain?

Once your website is ready, we’ll work with you step by step to connect your own domain name. It’s quick, simple, and we’ll guide you through the whole process so everything is set up correctly.

How long does a £50 website take to build?

Most £50 websites are completed within 1–3 days, depending on your content and requirements. Once we have your details, we move quickly and keep you updated throughout.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. If you already have a website, we can redesign it into a cleaner, more modern static site with improved layout and messaging.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website we build is mobile-first and responsive, so it looks great on phones, tablets, and desktops.
